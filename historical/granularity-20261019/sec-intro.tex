\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Public representations can encode useful geometry before any private task
labels are available. In community-content learning, users retain labelled
post histories and local models; a coordinator supplies a public feature map,
an unlabelled proxy and a class taxonomy. Protected aggregation can collect
bounded contributions without exposing individual reports~\cite{corriganGibbs2017prio,bittau2017prochlo,bonawitz2017practical}.
The learning question is what supervision those contributions should carry.
With strong public features, the choice is between releasing parameters,
querying every proxy example, and reusing one target over a semantic region.

\paragraph{The gap: release resolution needs a utility criterion.}
PATE aggregates teacher votes~\cite{papernot2017semi,papernot2018scalable},
and federated distillation transfers predictions on public
examples~\cite{li2019fedmd,lin2020feddf,itahara2021dsfl,abourayya2025little}.
A joint per-example vote report has replace-one whole-client sensitivity
$\sqrt{2|P|}$, even when related queries share a useful target.
A coarser release reduces sensitivity but can assign a common target to
examples from different classes. A release rule must account for both public
geometry and the noise that the actual cohort can average.
Public representations are an explicit resource: prior work establishes their
importance for private learning~\cite{tramer2021features,li2022large}.
Our question concerns the private supervision exchanged on top of them.

\paragraph{The idea: choose the unit of private supervision.}
Semantic-DP Distillation (SDD) forms $K$ public regions and releases one class
vote per client per region. Every proxy example inherits its region's target.
The report has $KC$ coordinates and tight whole-client sensitivity
$\sqrt{2K}$, independently of proxy size at fixed $K$.
A conditional quality model separates regional impurity from target decoding
and explains when additional budget stabilizes targets, when additional
regions supply useful resolution, and when the cohort cannot support that
resolution. A public-only predictor evaluates candidate $K$ without consulting
private margins or final-test labels. At $K=C$, weighted matching offers a
coverage-constrained decoder using the same protected matrix.

\paragraph{The evidence: compact supervision with a verified mechanism.}
At actual 10k strongly heterogeneous CIFAR-10 clients, $\varepsilon=0.1$ and
$\delta=10^{-5}$, a fixed $K=C=10$ matching decoder reaches
\textbf{$86.3\%$ accuracy without public task labels}, with five original and
five independent confirmation seeds. Its 100-coordinate report uses
\textbf{$200\times$ fewer coordinates} than per-example DP-FedCT.
On the original paired seeds, the gain over per-example transfer is
16.6 points ($95\%$ interval $[14.6,18.5]$); independent local learning gives
$42.4\%$. Replaying regional targets identifies the mechanism: matching
repairs repeated-class assignments in three of ten seeds and leaves the
other seven endpoints unchanged.

Matched partitions and query controls separate semantic reuse from simply
reducing the number of queries. A Gaussian approximation predicts regional
release-label quality across participation, resolution and privacy budgets,
validated against independent exact distributed noise. Distinct Usenet authors
and official Federated EMNIST writers supply natural client boundaries.
Together with a compact DP-Head baseline, these studies characterize which
released object the available geometry and aggregation resources support.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{A regional interface for whole-client private supervision.}
SDD reuses targets across public examples with a $KC$-coordinate report and
$\sqrt{2K}$ sensitivity. Established distributed integer noise protects its
adaptive transcript under an aggregate-only interface (\S\ref{sec:method}).
\item \textbf{A quality criterion for release granularity.}
An exact regional-label decomposition, an uncentered margin bound and an
independently checked Gaussian approximation connect public geometry to
noise-supported resolution (\S\ref{sec:quality}).
\item \textbf{Conditional decoding and empirical release choices.}
Paired decoder controls, independent seed confirmation, and natural client
studies identify when semantic reuse, class coverage and parameter averaging
supply useful private supervision (\S\ref{sec:eval}).
\end{itemize}
