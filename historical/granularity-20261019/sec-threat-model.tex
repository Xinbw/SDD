\subsection{Protected aggregate and whole-run client privacy}
\label{sec:threat}
\label{sec:threat:setting}

A fixed cohort of $n$ clients participates in each of $T$ rounds. Replace-one
adjacency changes the entire dataset of one honest client. Public features,
partitions and configurations are independent of private datasets. The
adversary sees the noisy sum and broadcasts; local models, individual reports
and honest noise shares remain hidden by the aggregation interface.

\paragraph{Aggregation assumptions.}
A1: secure additive aggregation reveals the sum and metadata that is public or
independent of protected data. A2: at most $t<n$ contributors disclose reports
and noise to the server, leaving $h=n-t$ honest independent shares.
A3: aggregation parties preserve that view against the stated coalition.
A4: honest contributors use bounded reports and fresh independent noise;
participation is fixed ($q=1$), with recalibration or abort if the required
honest cohort is unavailable. Secret-sharing or split-and-mix protocols can
instantiate the interface~\cite{bonawitz2017practical,corriganGibbs2017prio,ishai2006cryptography}.
Experiments implement protected-sum arithmetic; transport is a deployment
component. The guarantee concerns observation and collusion under A1--A4.

\begin{lemma}[Report sensitivity]
\label{lem:sens}
For $R,R'\in(\Delta^{C-1})^K$, $\|R-R'\|_2\le\sqrt{2K}$,
with equality for opposing one-hot rows. This holds for every public partition.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Integer sensitivity]
\label{lem:sens-int}
One-hot reports have $\Delta_q=s\sqrt{2K}$ exactly.
Rounded posteriors have $\Delta_q\le s\sqrt{2K}+\sqrt{KC}$.
A parameter update clipped at $B$ and guarded after rounding in the integer
ball $L_B=\lceil sB+\sqrt d/2\rceil$ has $\Delta_q\le2L_B$.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Release.}
Each client submits shares of $sR_i+G_i$ modulo $2^{64}$, with
$s=2^{16}$ and independent coordinates
$G_i[j]\sim\mathcal N_{\mathbb Z}(0,b^2)$.
The server projects the dequantised sum onto row simplexes and applies the
specified decoder. The exact discrete-Gaussian sampler and integer guards
are specified in Appendix~\ref{app:repro}.

\begin{theorem}[One protected aggregate]
\label{thm:round}
Under A1--A4, the aggregate in dimension $d$ is $\rho_r$-zCDP, where
\begin{equation}
\rho_r=\frac{\Delta_q^2}{2hb^2}+\frac{d\tau_h(b)}4,
\quad \tau_h(b)\le10(h-1)e^{-\pi^2b^2}.
\label{eq:round-rho}
\end{equation}
For $h=1$, $\tau_h=0$; Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} gives its exact expression.
\end{theorem}

This applies the convolution guarantee of Kairouz et
al.~\cite{kairouz2021distributed} to the regional integer query after the
coalition subtracts its disclosed shares. Additional honest noise, modular
addition, projection, matching and soft-target construction preserve privacy.

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive whole run]
\label{thm:run}
Conditionally calibrated releases compose to
$\rho_{\mathrm{total}}\le\sum_{r=1}^T\rho_r$.
The complete server transcript is $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP whenever
\begin{equation}
\rho_{\mathrm{total}}+2\sqrt{\rho_{\mathrm{total}}\log(1/\delta)}
\le\varepsilon.
\label{eq:accounting}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

Condition on data-independent training random tapes and each preceding public
transcript: another client's unchanged private data and that transcript fix its response;
the replaced client's report stays within the same bounded space.
Adaptive composition therefore includes every broadcast~\cite{bun2016concentrated}.
Public-only design consumes no client budget. Private local models are not
released. Test metrics and clean-count diagnostics are offline measurements,
outside the deployment transcript claimed to satisfy this guarantee.

Writing $L=\log(1/\delta)$ and
$\rho_\varepsilon=(\sqrt{L+\varepsilon}-\sqrt L)^2$,
we round $b$ upward from $\Delta_q/\sqrt{2h\rho_\varepsilon/T}$ and verify
Eq.~\eqref{eq:round-rho} including its convolution correction.
Every release retains a numerical certificate with actual $n$, honest $h$,
integer sensitivity, noise scale and composed budget. The averaging denominator
and calibration always use the same participating population.
For $t=0$, mean-noise standard deviation is bounded by
\begin{equation}
v=\frac{b\sqrt n}{sn}\approx\frac{\sqrt{TK/\rho_\varepsilon}}{n}.
\label{eq:design-budget}
\end{equation}
This scale connects release resolution to target quality in \S\ref{sec:quality}.
