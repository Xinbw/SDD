\section{Proofs and Integer Calibration}
\label{app:proofs}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:sens}]
Squared Euclidean distance decomposes over rows:
$\|R-R'\|_2^2=\sum_{k=1}^K\|R[k,\cdot]-R'[k,\cdot]\|_2^2$.
For simplex vectors $p,p'$, $\|p\|_2^2\le1$, $\|p'\|_2^2\le1$ and
$\langle p,p'\rangle\ge0$, so $\|p-p'\|_2^2\le2$.
Distinct one-hot rows attain equality. Repeating them across all $K$ rows
attains $2K$. Statistical independence between rows is unnecessary.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:sens-int}]
Write $\operatorname{round}(sR)=sR+e$, with $|e_j|\le1/2$.
Then $\|e-e'\|_2\le\sqrt d$, giving
$\Delta_q\le s\sqrt{2K}+\sqrt d$. One-hot votes have zero rounding error.
Two updates clipped to a radius-$B$ ball differ by at most $2B$,
giving $2sB+\sqrt d$ after rounding. The implemented guard confines
integer reports to radius $L_B=\lceil sB+\sqrt d/2\rceil$, hence sensitivity
$2L_B$. It checks squared integer norms exactly in the recorded numeric
range and replaces an out-of-ball report with zero.
Each conditional update is clipped relative to the same preceding public broadcast.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorems~\ref{thm:round} and~\ref{thm:run}]
Condition on a public transcript and a coalition's disclosed contributions.
The remaining integer query has sensitivity $\Delta_q$ and retains at least
$h$ independent scale-$b$ noise shares per coordinate.
Proposition~10 of Kairouz et al.~\cite{kairouz2021distributed} gives the
zCDP upper bound in Eq.~\eqref{eq:round-rho}. Its first branch suffices for
all reported certificates. Since $j/(j+1)\ge1/2$,
$\tau_h\le10(h-1)e^{-\pi^2b^2}$.
Modulo reduction and later target construction are post-processing.
The same conditional bound holds at every round, so adaptive composition
sums the bounds. Equation~\eqref{eq:accounting} converts the sum to
client-level $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Strict numerical slack.}
The implementation increases the integer scale beyond the Gaussian reference
and verifies $\Delta_q^2/(2hb^2)+d\tau_h/4\le\rho_\varepsilon/T$.
It stores $\log[10(h-1)e^{-\pi^2b^2}]$ and conservatively uses
$10^{-30}$ as an upper bound when the exponential is smaller.
Thus underflow never silently discards the correction.
The experiment artifacts certify each stage and its composed budget.
For $10\%$ collusion tolerance, both $h$ and its correction are recomputed.
Ninety-digit recomputation verifies all composed certificate bounds; the
largest retained convolution upper bound is $10^{-30}$.

\paragraph{Ring arithmetic.}
Reports and shares are added modulo $2^{64}$; the signed representative is
dequantised for learning. Privacy is preserved under this modular map even
when wraparound occurs. Utility wraparound is controlled using the
sub-Gaussian tail of the discrete Gaussian~\cite{kairouz2021distributed}:
for a scalar noise sum $G$, $\Pr(|G|\ge a)\le2e^{-a^2/(2nb^2)}$.
A union bound across released coordinates and rounds, with signal bounded by
the quantised report range, certifies the recorded configurations.
Its log probability is below $-3.5\times10^{18}$ across primary releases.

\section{Reproducibility and Public Selection}
\label{app:repro}

The anonymous supplementary archive has a root \texttt{README.md} and
\texttt{reproduce.py}. Run \texttt{python reproduce.py verify} to check
inputs, frozen configurations, all numerical tables and stored outputs;
\texttt{python reproduce.py smoke} replays representative fixed cases.
\texttt{EXPERIMENT\_INDEX.csv} maps each condition to its seeds, configuration,
role and result. Feature bundles, candidate scores, raw logs, exact integer
certificates and complete grid tables are included. Public selection omits test
arrays; configuration hashes are checked against the frozen manifests.

The frozen image extractor is DINOv2 ViT-S/14 from the official public
checkpoint~\cite{oquab2023dinov2}; its hash is recorded. All images use
$224\times224$ RGB bicubic resizing and the checkpoint's normalisation.
PCA and feature normalisation are fixed public preprocessing; application to
private examples is point-wise. Public $K$-means uses five initialisations,
at most $100$ iterations, seed $1729$, and deterministic nonempty-cluster repair.
Random grouping preserves the semantic partition's exact region sizes.
The feature control changes the partition to raw-pixel PCA while retaining
the same client learner and feature map. The representative control queries
the proxy point closest to each public centroid.

Public pilot teachers use seed $1729$, disjoint development data, and the same
round-1 learner as the final experiment. Candidate $K$ and correction modes
use mean-posterior opinions and are scored by nearest-public-centroid label
transfer on validation data,
averaged over noise seeds $91001$--$91005$. Exact ties prefer smaller $K$,
then ordinary consensus. Six DP-Head clipping candidates are public norm
quantiles $0.5,0.75,0.9,0.95,0.99,1.0$; they use five noise repeats.
PATE-DI settings use two fixed noise repeats per public candidate.
These public design surrogates neither use private client data nor consume
their privacy budget. Per-budget choices are retained alongside every candidate
score, enabling independent verification of the selection rule.
The representative comparisons retain those same $K$/consensus choices;
no private result selects between M and R. The no-public-label check instead
uses fixed taxonomy resolution and centered consensus. Proxy labels enter its
offline diagnostics, while training targets come from the protected client sum.

\paragraph{Exact noise and experimental replay.}
The sampler uses a discrete-Laplace rejection proposal and rational
Bernoulli $\exp(-\gamma)$ decisions as in Canonne et al.~\cite{canonne2020discrete}.
Rejection from ideal independent random bits and arbitrary-precision fallbacks
retain the exact integer distribution and its tail. The executable uses
ChaCha20 pseudorandom bit streams under the corresponding cryptographic
random-source assumption, with fresh secret keys drawn from the operating
system for deployment. The privacy proof uses independent private random coins.
Recorded benchmark seeds reproduce offline utility calculations and diagnostics,
rather than defining a publicly seeded deployment privacy transcript.
Sampler checks compare zero-event probabilities, moments and tails with
analytic integer-Gaussian probabilities; the learner is checked against scalar
SGD and one-client noninterference before final tests.

\paragraph{Resource and protocol scope.}
Experiments use one RTX 5090 GPU and $16$ CPU noise-sampling threads.
Vectorisation trains every client independently and samples every client's
share; cached round-1 states accelerate paired comparisons.
Private models are not transmitted. The utility simulator evaluates the noisy
sum directly and assumes the protected transport of A1--A4.
Reported clean pseudo-label metrics and test accuracies are offline benchmark
measurements, outside the claimed deployment transcript.

\section{Baseline Accounting and Training}
\label{app:baselines}

For DP-FedCT, the public singleton partition is exactly the per-example vote
interface. SDD and this variant share initial local states, private batches,
local optimiser, budget conversion and post-processing.
For DP-Head, each client clips its update relative to the preceding public
global head and enforces the public integer-ball radius after rounding; the server averages the protected integer updates, broadcasts
the new head and generates proxy targets from this public model.
All clients adopt that head and reset momentum. Each round still has $60$
local steps. We evaluate both the final broadcast and the clients immediately before that
broadcast, retaining the first two protected heads, with no extra local steps.
The third release remains charged for both endpoints.

For PATE-DI, let $v(x)$ be the aggregate class-count vector.
Protected screening forms noisy $\max_c v_c(x)$ for all $|P|$ queries.
Its replace-one $L_2$ sensitivity is $\sqrt{|P|}$.
Thresholds are $0.1,0.2,0.3$ in mean-vote units; answer caps are
$0.25|P|$ or $0.5|P|$; screening receives $0.25$ or $0.5$ of the per-round
privacy allowance. The remaining allowance answers at most $M$ queries with
joint sensitivity $\sqrt{2M}$. Thresholding and selecting the highest noisy
confidences are post-processing of screening. The answer sensitivity holds
conditionally on every selected subset. Both stages use the corrected
distributed integer-noise bound and compose over $T$ rounds.
The tighter data-independent R\'enyi conversion~\cite{balle2020hypothesis} is
\begin{equation}
\varepsilon\le\inf_{a>1}\left[
a\rho_{\rm total}+\frac{\log(1/(a\delta))}{a-1}
+\log(1-1/a)\right].
\end{equation}
This conversion is granted to PATE-DI in addition to protected screening.
The evaluated view includes noisy confidences and selected noisy count vectors.
Data-dependent PATE accounting requires additional protected analysis of
private consensus and accounting outputs; it is a distinct protocol scope.

\paragraph{Original-architecture corroboration.}
The full ResNet-18 diagnostic uses actual/calibrated $n=100$, CIFAR-10
$\alpha=0.1$ and $6000$ replacement draws per client. Two continuation
rounds each use $20$ epochs; the three protected releases use
$\varepsilon=0.1,1$. It fixes $K=10$ and uses a singleton partition
for DP-FedCT. Both use the same local GPU trainer, exact noise, and individually trained cached seed-$42$ round-1 states. Evaluation uses the proxy-excluded first
half of test. At $\varepsilon=0.1$, regional/per-example accuracy is $22.3/25.5\%$;
at $1$, it is $38.9/25.5\%$. These architecture-specific operating points
are separate from the main frozen-feature population study.
