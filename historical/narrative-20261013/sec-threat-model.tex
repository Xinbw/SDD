\section{Threat Model and Privacy Guarantee}
\label{sec:threat}

\subsection{Private units and the aggregation interface}
\label{sec:threat:setting}

Client $i$ holds its entire local dataset $D_i$ and model $f_i$. A fixed cohort
of $n$ clients participates in each of $T$ rounds. The public proxy $P$, its
partition, feature extractor and release configuration are fixed independently
of these private datasets. The adversary observes the noisy sum and every
broadcast; local models, individual reports and honest noise shares remain
on the client side of the aggregation interface.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
\item[A1 (aggregate-only view).] Secure additive aggregation reveals the
sum of contributions and protocol metadata that is public or independent
of protected data, while hiding individual values.
Secret sharing or split-and-mix aggregation can instantiate this
interface~\cite{bonawitz2017practical,corriganGibbs2017prio,ishai2006cryptography}.
The privacy accountant operates on the released sum.
\item[A2 (bounded collusion).] At most $t<n$ contributors disclose their
reports and noise to the server; at least $h=n-t$ independent honest shares
remain. The main evaluation uses $t=0$ and separately tests $t=0.1n$.
\item[A3 (protected aggregation).] Aggregation parties maintain A1 against
the server and the stated coalition. The learning experiment implements the
sum arithmetic; secure transport is an assumed deployment component.
\item[A4 (compliant contributions).] Honest clients use the bounded report
and fresh independent noise. Participation is fixed ($q=1$); a deployment
rechecks calibration or aborts if the required honest cohort is unavailable.
The guarantee concerns observation and collusion under this interface.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Replace-one client adjacency.}
Adjacent inputs replace the entire dataset of one honest client. Conditioned
on any previous public transcript, that client's report may change arbitrarily
within the bounded report space. Other clients use the same transcript and
their own local data. Conditional sensitivity and adaptive composition
therefore protect the complete server transcript, even though reports evolve
over rounds. All evaluated population sizes are actual reporting populations:
the averaging denominator and noise calibration use the same $n$.

\subsection{A bounded integer release}
\label{sec:threat:protocol}

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{One round of Semantic-DP Distillation}
\label{alg:protocol}\small
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\State \textbf{client $i$:}
\State \quad $\tilde R_i\gets$ regional posteriors (mean or representative)
\State \quad $R_i[k,\cdot]\gets e_{\arg\max_c\tilde R_i[k,c]}$
\State \quad $Z_i\gets sR_i$ \Comment{exact integer votes}
\State \quad $G_i[j]\gets\mathcal N_{\mathbb Z}(0,b^2)$ independently
\State \quad submit shares of $Y_i=Z_i+G_i$ modulo $2^{64}$
\State \textbf{server:}
\State \quad $S\gets\sum_iY_i$ \Comment{aggregate-only view}
\State \quad $A\gets\operatorname{Proj}_{\Delta}(S/(sn))$ row-wise
\State \quad extract cluster targets by fixed public post-processing
\State \quad broadcast targets for hard-label local distillation
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

Here $\mathcal N_{\mathbb Z}(0,b^2)$ has mass proportional to
$\exp(-z^2/(2b^2))$ on integers. Its scale parameter bounds its standard
deviation; its variance is strictly below $b^2$~\cite{kairouz2021distributed}.
The implementation uses the rejection sampler of Canonne et
al.~\cite{canonne2020discrete}, with rational Bernoulli decisions.
Modular addition, dequantisation, projection and target extraction are
post-processing. Appendix~\ref{app:repro} specifies the sampler and replay protocol.

\begin{lemma}[Report sensitivity]
\label{lem:sens}
For $R_i,R_i'\in(\Delta^{C-1})^K$,
$\|R_i-R_i'\|_2\le\sqrt{2K}$, and this report-space bound is tight.
It holds for every fixed public partition, including random partitions.
\end{lemma}

Each simplex row has diameter $\sqrt2$; opposing one-hot rows attain the
$2K$ squared bound. Semantic controls separately test learning quality.

\begin{lemma}[Quantised sensitivity]
\label{lem:sens-int}
For one-hot reports, $\Delta_q=s\sqrt{2K}$ exactly. For rounded posterior
reports in dimension $d=KC$,
$\Delta_q\le s\sqrt{2K}+\sqrt d$. For an update clipped at norm $B$,
$\Delta_q\le2sB+\sqrt d$.
\end{lemma}

Two rounding-error vectors differ by at most one per coordinate, hence
by at most $\sqrt d$ in norm. For the clipping baseline, define the integer radius
$L_B=\lceil sB+\sqrt d/2\rceil$ and enforce this ball after rounding;
its certified sensitivity is $2L_B$.
The integer guard includes any floating clipping error.
The main vote interface incurs no rounding slack. Sensitivity depends on
the emitted object, independently of how a client trains its model.

\subsection{Explicit convolution and whole-run accounting}
\label{sec:threat:guarantee}

\begin{theorem}[One aggregate release]
\label{thm:round}
Under A1--A4, let $h=n-t$, $b\ge1$, and
\begin{align}
\tau_h(b)&=10\sum_{j=1}^{h-1}
 \exp\!\left(-2\pi^2b^2\frac{j}{j+1}\right),\label{eq:tau}\\
\rho_r&=\frac{\Delta_q^2}{2hb^2}+\frac{d\tau_h(b)}4.
\label{eq:round-rho}
\end{align}
The aggregate is $\rho_r$-zCDP for replace-one client adjacency. The empty
sum is zero when $h=1$; otherwise
$\tau_h(b)\le10(h-1)e^{-\pi^2b^2}$.
\end{theorem}

After subtracting colluding contributions, $h$ independent discrete-Gaussian
shares protect the integer query. Equation~\eqref{eq:round-rho} is the first
branch of Proposition~10 in Kairouz et al.~\cite{kairouz2021distributed},
with the actual replace-one sensitivity $\Delta_q$. Additional honest noise
and modular reduction preserve this guarantee. This bound accounts for the
convolution distribution directly.

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive whole run]
\label{thm:run}
For $T$ conditionally calibrated releases,
$\rho_{\mathrm{total}}\le\sum_{r=1}^T\rho_r$. The server transcript is
$(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP whenever
\begin{equation}
\rho_{\mathrm{total}}+2\sqrt{\rho_{\mathrm{total}}\log(1/\delta)}
\le\varepsilon.\label{eq:accounting}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

The composition includes all broadcasts~\cite{bun2016concentrated}.
Public feature extraction, public-only configuration selection and
distillation of released labels consume no additional client budget.
Offline benchmark accuracy and clean-report diagnostics measure the mechanism;
they are not additional outputs claimed to satisfy its privacy guarantee.

\paragraph{Calibration that can be checked numerically.}
Writing $L=\log(1/\delta)$, set
$\rho_\varepsilon=(\sqrt{L+\varepsilon}-\sqrt L)^2$ and choose
\begin{equation}
b=\left\lfloor\frac{\Delta_q}{\sqrt{2h\rho_\varepsilon/T}}\right\rfloor+1.
\label{eq:integer-scale}
\end{equation}
We then verify the full corrected bound, including $d\tau_h/4$, against
$\rho_\varepsilon/T$. The upward integer step provides slack; every
experimental release has $\tau_h<10^{-30}$. Machine-readable certificates
record $n,h,b,\Delta_q,d,\log\tau_h$ and the recomposed privacy bound.
For $K=10$, $T=3$, $\varepsilon=0.1$, $\delta=10^{-5}$, the continuous
reference scale on the sum is $372.50$: residual scales are approximately
$3.725$, $0.3725$ and $0.03725$ at actual $n=100$, $1000$ and $10000$.
These are different participating populations in the learning experiment.

\paragraph{Participation and resolution.}
Since $\operatorname{Var}(G_i[j])<b^2$, the full-cohort mean has per-coordinate
standard deviation bounded by $b\sqrt n/(sn)$. With $t=0$, this is close to
\begin{equation}
\frac{\sigma}{n},\qquad
\sigma=\sqrt{TK/\rho_\varepsilon}.\label{eq:design-budget}
\end{equation}
At a fixed accountant, per-example and cluster votes therefore have noise-scale
ratio $\sqrt{|P|/K}$ up to integer calibration. A larger cohort supplies more
reports as well as a larger averaging denominator. Its evolving consensus is
measured at each actual population size in the scaling experiment.

\paragraph{Collusion tolerance.}
Replacing $n$ with $h=n-t$ in calibration retains the corrected guarantee
after the coalition subtracts its shares. With all $n$ shares present, the
noise-scale upper bound increases by approximately $\sqrt{n/(n-t)}$,
or $1.054$ for $10\%$ collusion tolerance. Integer rounding and
$\tau_h$ remain in each certificate. This quantifies the extra noise required
by the stated coalition model; the paired utility experiment measures
its learning effect.
