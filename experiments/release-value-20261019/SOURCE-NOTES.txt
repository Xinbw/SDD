Execution and analytical provenance: private-supervision value/verification study

PRIVATE-VALIDATION-DESIGN.json froze ten actual holdout cohorts, fresh public
label draws, restricted candidate family, total budgets and 50/25/25 rho split
before these experiments. The source file private_validation.py remains byte
identical to its recorded execution hash. All 3000 original decisions and
coordinate-band outputs are immutable, including every rejected case.

LINEAR-BANDS-DESIGN.json is an analytical addition after initial coordinate
bands were evaluated. It fixes direct sum-numerator/denominator concentration
bounds of the SAME protected histogram. The targets are the fixed C classes;
no threshold or weight search is used, no private query is repeated, and all
initial records remain separate. Source/test statistics do not choose a rule.
Finite-noise coverage follows the exact sub-Gaussian proxies of these linear
functions; existing deployment budgets do not change.

NO-FEEDBACK-DESIGN.json fixes three private-only training rounds on original
600-record cohorts, with matched source/noise tapes. First and final private
clean votes match preceding T=1 sources; first protected matrices match T=3
feedback sources exactly. Last and mean-of-three readouts are both reported.
The mean restores the frozen-query noise scale while changing teacher signal;
its performance gap is not identified as a pure budget-splitting cost.

ZERO-LABEL-POOL-AUDIT.json records postprocessing of 110 existing protected
point matrices. Its outcomes were already inspected when the audit replay was
frozen; it is not called a prospective experiment. PUBLIC-MAPS-DESIGN.json
records a label-free distance replay map after experiments for CPU verification.
Neither operation queries private models or adds privacy charge.
